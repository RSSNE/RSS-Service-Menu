Support Guidance

Specialist Support Available

All support is free and confidential. Engagement timings below are guidance — contact the RSS as early as possible for all programmes.

PPIE & Co-Design

Patient and public involvement and engagement strategy, co-design workshops, lived experience integration, and endpoint development with communities.

⏱ Engage from conception — PPIE must be built into research design, not added at submission

Clinical Trials Methodology

Trial design (RCT, pragmatic, adaptive), protocol development, feasibility and pilot study planning, MHRA/CTR considerations, and site selection support.

⏱ Engage at study design stage — at least 3–4 months before submission

Statistics & Data Science

Sample size calculation, statistical analysis plan, power calculations, data management, routine data and EHR linkage, real-world evidence design.

⏱ Engage at study design stage — at least 3 months before submission

Health Economics

Economic model development (CEA, CUA, CBA), cost-effectiveness alongside trial, budget impact analysis, NICE HTA modelling, and NHS adoption evidence.

⏱ Must be built in from conception — not retrospective. Engage at earliest opportunity

Full Application Review

Detailed peer review of full draft application including narrative, economic case, methodology, PPIE section, and lay summary. Prioritised feedback aligned to scoring criteria.

⏱ Submit draft to RSS 4–6 weeks before funding deadline

Summary Review

Light-touch review of near-final application focusing on clarity, impact statement, lay summary, and headline scoring criteria. Rapid turnaround.

⏱ Submit draft to RSS 2 weeks before funding deadline

⚠ Social Care SME Pathway — Important Note

Community Interest Companies (CICs), social enterprises, and social entrepreneurs in the Health and Life Sciences sector ARE eligible SMEs where they meet the SME definition (<250 employees, <€50m turnover). SSCR, PHR, RfPB, i4i, and Innovation Catalyst are all open to this cohort. Contact the RSS for a scoping consultation to confirm eligibility and identify the best route.
